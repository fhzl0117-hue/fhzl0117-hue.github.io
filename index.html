About

What K-Wire Network is — and isn't.

One format, several artists

Every site in the network follows the same rules: news is translated and always linked to its original source, reviews are text-only, video is official-channel-only, and release dates convert automatically to the visitor's timezone.

Independently run

Each wire is an independent fan project. None of them are affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any label, agency, or artist named on this page.

Growing slowly

New desks are added deliberately, one artist at a time, once there's enough real, sourced content to sustain one — not on a fixed schedule.

K-Wire Network

A directory for a family of independent K-pop fan wires — built to translate, schedule, and curate, never to reproduce.
